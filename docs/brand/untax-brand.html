Get your ADHD tax back.

Untax catches your free trials before they charge you, and gets you unstuck when you freeze. Every win lands on a receipt called ADHD Tax Refunded. It was called Parachute; that name is retired from everything a user sees.

  • Write the wordmark as lowercase untax, and "Untax" in sentences. Never UnTax, UNTAX, or Un-tax.
  • Leave clear space of at least a quarter of the icon's width. The mark's minimum size is 20pt; the wordmark's is 120pt wide.

Shape

Soft, flat, roomy

  • 8pt grid. Screen padding 20; stack spacing 12, 16, and 24.
  • Radius 14 on buttons and cards. Receipt surfaces get a torn bottom edge instead.
  • One elevation: a hairline border in 8% Ink. No drop shadows, except on the celebration share card.
  • Touch targets are at least 44pt. Primary buttons are 54pt tall.
